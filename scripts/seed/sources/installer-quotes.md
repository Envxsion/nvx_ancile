## Notes from the visits

**Northern Heat Co.** were thorough and brought their own heat loss figures, which matched the survey within 0.3 kW. They proposed a 10 kW unit "for headroom", which is above the survey's recommendation.

**Aire Valley Renewables** were the cheapest and could start soonest. Their 8.5 kW unit is slightly below the 9 kW recommendation, and they want to replace six radiators, two more than the survey says are needed. They were vague about the flow temperature they would commission at.

**Brightwater Energy** were the only installer to include under-floor insulation, which the survey recommends. Their quote is the most expensive but includes about £1,900 of insulation work that the others leave out.
