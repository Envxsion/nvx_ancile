# The Salt Road: outline (acts and turns)

## Act one: the causeway (chapters 1 to 6)

Mara arrives, is stranded by the tide and spends the night at the Ship. She meets Callum. The next day she meets Eleanor Pyke, who offers a generous price for the salt pan land "on behalf of a client". Mara refuses without knowing why. At the cottage she finds the locked tin box under the floor of the larder.

Turn: the key to the tin box is not in the house. Callum's father had it.

## Act two: the pans (chapters 7 to 15)

Mara and Callum clear the sluice and find that it was deliberately blocked with concrete, not left to silt. The tin box holds her grandfather's tide log for the last month of his life, with the night he drowned marked "they moved the posts".

Midpoint: Mara learns that the causeway posts were moved the week he died, on the developer's predecessor's instructions, to make the salt pans look cut off and worthless.

Turn: Eleanor's client is revealed. It is the same family.

## Act three: the tide (chapters 16 to 22)

Mara restores the pans to prove the land is workable, which makes it worth more and the old fraud provable. A storm surge threatens the island; her flood work and the old salt pan sluice together save the low cottages at Ness End. Callum's father admits what he saw.

Ending: Mara does not sell. She stays through one winter "to see the pans through", which the reader understands to mean longer.
