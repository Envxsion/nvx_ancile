# Launch kick-off: meeting notes

22 September, 10:00 to 11:15. Present: Priya (product marketing), Tom (engineering lead), Aisha (design), Marcus (partnerships), Hannah (support).

## Decisions

- Launch date confirmed as 18 November. No soft launch; the beta community gets a 24-hour head start instead.
- Calm Routes is the only feature named in the headline. Alerts and shared commute go in the body copy.
- The launch offer is a 30-day free trial of Plus, not a discount.

## Discussion

Tom raised that the routing service needs a load test at five times beta traffic before launch. The last test reached three times beta traffic before latency passed two seconds. He wants a week for the work.

Aisha showed two directions for the App Store screenshots: one led by the map, one led by the score badge. The room preferred the score badge; it explains Calm Routes without words.

Marcus has three employers interested in Teams pilots: a hospital trust in Bristol, a university in Leeds and an engineering firm in Manchester. The hospital needs a data protection impact assessment before signing.

Hannah asked for a help centre article on how Calm Routes scores are calculated, ready before launch. Support expects most tickets in the first week to ask why a route is "slower".
