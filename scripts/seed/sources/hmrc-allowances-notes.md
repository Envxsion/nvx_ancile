# Sole trader tax notes, 2025 to 2026

My own notes, checked against HMRC's pages in April.

## Allowable expenses

You can claim costs that are wholly and exclusively for the business. Where something is used partly for personal reasons, such as home broadband, you can claim the business share if you can show a reasonable basis for it.

Not allowable: everyday meals while working from your usual place of work, clothing that is not a uniform or protective, and fines.

## Equipment

Equipment such as a laptop or drawing tablet can be claimed in full in the year you buy it under the Annual Investment Allowance, as long as you use cash basis or claim capital allowances. If you also use it personally, claim only the business proportion.

## Working from home

Either claim a flat rate (£10 a month for 25 to 50 hours of home working a month, £18 for 51 to 100 hours, £26 for 101 hours or more), or work out the actual business share of household costs. You cannot do both for the same costs.

## Payments on account

If your Self Assessment bill is more than £1,000 and less than 80% of your tax was collected at source, you make two payments on account towards next year's bill: each is half of this year's bill, due 31 January and 31 July. A balancing payment for any difference is due the following 31 January.

## Key dates for this tax year

- Tax year ends 5 April 2026.
- Online return and balancing payment due 31 January 2027.
- First payment on account for 2026 to 2027 also due 31 January 2027.
