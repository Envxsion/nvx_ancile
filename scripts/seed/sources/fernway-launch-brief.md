# Fernway 2.0 launch brief (v3)

Owner: Priya Nair, product marketing. Last edited 29 September.

## What we are launching

Fernway 2.0 is the route planner for people who cycle to work. The headline feature is **Calm Routes**: routes that trade a few minutes for quieter roads, fewer junctions and less climbing, scored from council traffic counts and our own ride data. Version 2.0 also adds live disruption alerts for roadworks and closures, and a shared commute mode so colleagues can meet on the way in.

Launch date is **Tuesday 18 November**. The App Store and Google Play listings go live at 07:00 GMT, timed for the morning commute.

## Who it is for

The primary audience is the regular commuter: someone who rides three or more days a week, mostly in towns and cities, and who already uses a maps app but finds it sends them down main roads. The beta told us this group cares about safety first, predictability second and speed third.

The secondary audience is employers who run cycle-to-work schemes. They buy Fernway Teams for staff and want to see uptake, not individual journeys.

## Goals for the first 90 days

- 40,000 new installs, of which 35% ride at least once in their first week.
- 2,500 paying subscribers on Fernway Plus.
- Five employer pilots on Fernway Teams, each with at least 50 seats.
- An App Store rating of 4.6 or higher.

## Pricing (proposed)

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | £0 | £0 | Standard routes, three saved routes |
| Plus | £4.99 | £39.99 | Calm Routes, alerts, unlimited saved routes |
| Teams | £3.50 per seat | £36 per seat | Minimum 20 seats, admin dashboard |

The annual Plus price is a 33% discount on paying monthly. Finance has asked that we do not discount further at launch; a launch offer should be an extended trial rather than a lower price.

## Risks

- Calm Routes scoring depends on traffic counts that are out of date in some councils. We will label scores as "estimated" where the data is older than two years.
- Apple's review times have been running at four to six days. The build must be submitted by 10 November.
- The press embargo lifts at 07:00 on launch day; anyone who breaks it loses early access to future launches.

## Channels

Owned: the beta community (about 6,200 riders), the newsletter (31,000 subscribers), and in-app messages to existing users. Earned: cycling press and two commuter podcasts. Paid: a small test on Instagram and Strava, capped at £8,000 for the launch month.
