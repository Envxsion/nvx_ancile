# Pricing research summary: Fernway Plus

Prepared by the growth team from the beta pricing survey (n = 1,184) and six follow-up interviews, September.

## Willingness to pay

Riders were shown four monthly prices for Calm Routes and alerts. The share who said they would "probably" or "definitely" subscribe was:

- £2.99 a month: 41%
- £3.99 a month: 34%
- £4.99 a month: 29%
- £5.99 a month: 17%

The drop between £4.99 and £5.99 is the steepest in the curve. Revenue per 100 respondents peaks at £4.99.

## Monthly or annual first

When the annual price was shown first, 46% of those who chose Plus picked annual. When monthly was shown first, only 22% picked annual. Interviewees described the annual price as "the grown-up option" when it came first, and as "a commitment" when it came second.

## Trials

A 14-day trial converted at 18% in the beta. A 30-day trial converted at 21%, but cost twice as much in free usage. Interviewees said a month felt fair for something that depends on the weather: "two weeks of rain and you've learned nothing".

## What people would pay more for

The most requested paid features after Calm Routes were offline maps (named by 38%), a winter mode that avoids roads not gritted by the council (27%), and route sharing with family (19%).

## Competitor reference points

Most mainstream cycling apps charge between £3.99 and £7.99 a month. None offers a quiet-road score; two offer "low traffic" toggles that respondents found unreliable.
