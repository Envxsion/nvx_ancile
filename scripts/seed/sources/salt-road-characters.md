# The Salt Road: character bible

## Mara Wren (protagonist)

Thirty years old. A hydrologist working for a consultancy in Rotterdam, specialising in tidal flood defences. Left Holy Island (fictionalised as "the island") at nineteen and has not been back. Precise, guarded, quick to assume she is being managed. Her motive across the book: she believes her grandfather's drowning was not an accident, and that the salt pans are why. She wants the truth more than she wants the cottage, and she tells herself she will sell the cottage as soon as she has it.

## Agnes Wren (her grandmother, dead before the book opens)

Kept the island's only shop for forty years. Let the salt pans go to ruin after her husband died. Left Mara the cottage and a locked tin box that the solicitor has not been told about.

## Callum Reid

The man in the car. Thirty-one. Runs boat trips for birdwatchers and repairs nets. His father was the coastguard on duty the night Mara's grandfather drowned. Kind, slow to speak, protective of the island's quiet. He knows more than he says about the sluice.

## Eleanor Pyke

The solicitor in Alnmouth. Sixty, brisk, an incomer of thirty years' standing who is still called an incomer. Acting for a developer who wants to buy the salt pan land for a "heritage salt works" visitor centre. She has not declared this interest to Mara.

## Rules for the book

- Mara never cries in front of anyone on the island.
- The tide times in the book follow real tables for the year it is set (1998).
- Nobody says "salt" in Agnes's hearing in any flashback.
