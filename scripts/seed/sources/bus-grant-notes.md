# Boiler Upgrade Scheme: notes

Notes taken from the Ofgem guidance pages and a call with Northern Heat Co., September.

- The Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS) gives a grant of **£7,500** towards an air-source heat pump in England and Wales.
- The installer applies for the grant, not the homeowner, and takes it off the invoice. You never handle the money.
- The installer must be MCS certified. All three quotes are from MCS-certified firms.
- The property needs a valid Energy Performance Certificate with no outstanding recommendations for loft or cavity wall insulation. Our EPC is from 2019 and lists no outstanding insulation recommendations.
- The heat pump must replace a fossil fuel system: our gas combination boiler qualifies.
- Once the installer applies, the voucher is valid for three months for an air-source heat pump. If the install slips beyond that, the installer has to reapply.
- The grant cannot be combined with other government funding for the same heat pump, but it can sit alongside separate funding for insulation.
